How to Apply — Step by Step

1

Check Eligibility

Verify that you meet the minimum academic qualifications and any discipline-specific requirements. See the Eligibility Criteria page for full details.

2

Identify Research Area & Supervisor

Browse Research Areas and Supervisors. It is strongly recommended to contact a potential supervisor before applying to discuss research alignment and vacancy.

3

Fill & Submit Application

Download Form PhD-01 (Application for Admission) or apply through the university's online portal. Attach all required documents and pay the application fee.

4

Entrance Test / Exemption

Appear for the university-level Research Entrance Test (RET). Candidates holding UGC-NET/JRF, CSIR-NET/JRF, GATE, or equivalent qualifying exams are exempted from the entrance test.

5

Research Proposal Presentation

Shortlisted candidates present a brief research proposal (10–15 minutes) before the Doctoral Research Committee (DRC) of the respective department.

6

Interview & Selection

Selected candidates attend a personal interview. Final selection is based on entrance test score (if applicable), research proposal quality, and interview performance.

7

Offer Letter & Enrolment

Successful candidates receive an offer letter. Complete the enrolment formalities, submit original documents, and pay the first semester fee to confirm admission.

8

Supervisor Allocation & Coursework

The DRC formally allocates the Research Supervisor. Scholars begin the mandatory coursework in their first year as per the Ph.D. Regulations.

Documents Required

  • Completed application form (PhD-01)
  • 10th & 12th mark sheets
  • UG & PG degree certificates & mark sheets
  • NET/GATE/JRF certificate (if applicable)
  • Experience certificates (for part-time applicants)
  • Research proposal (1–2 pages)
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Passport-size photographs (4 copies)
  • ID proof (Aadhaar / Passport)
  • Application fee receipt
