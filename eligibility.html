Eligibility Criteria

Minimum qualifications for Ph.D. admission across all schools.

Admissions
Admission Process Eligibility Criteria Ph.D. Regulations Application Forms
General Rule: Candidates must hold a Master's degree (or equivalent) with a minimum of 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD as per UGC norms) from a recognized university.

General Eligibility

CategoryMinimum QualificationMinimum Marks
General / OBC (Creamy Layer)Master's degree in relevant discipline55%
SC / ST / OBC-NCL / PwDMaster's degree in relevant discipline50%
M.Phil. holdersM.Phil. degree from recognized university55%
Professional degree holdersB.E./B.Tech. with PG / relevant professional degreeAs per UGC norms

School-Wise Eligibility

⚙️ Engineering

  • M.E. / M.Tech. in relevant branch (preferred)
  • M.Sc. in relevant science subject with 55% marks
  • B.E./B.Tech. with GATE score (for direct Ph.D. entry — limited seats)

🔬 Science

  • M.Sc. in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biotechnology, or Environmental Science
  • NET/GATE qualified candidates preferred

📊 Management

  • MBA / M.Com. / M.A. in relevant subject with 55%
  • CA / ICWA / CS with 55% in final examination also eligible
  • Part-time applicants need minimum 2 years of relevant work experience

💊 Pharmacy

  • M.Pharm. in relevant specialization with 55%
  • GPAT qualified candidates given preference

🏥 Allied Health Sciences

  • Master's degree in relevant health science discipline
  • Valid registration with respective state/national council mandatory
  • Minimum 1 year clinical/practical experience preferred

📚 Humanities & Social Sciences

  • M.A. in relevant discipline with 55%
  • UGC-NET qualified candidates exempted from entrance test

Entrance Test Exemptions

The following candidates are exempted from the university Research Entrance Test (RET):

UGC-NET / JRF CSIR-NET / JRF GATE (valid score) ICMR-JRF DBT-JRF GPAT SLET / SET M.Phil. holders DST-INSPIRE Fellows
