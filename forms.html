🔗 INFLIBNET Research Resources

National platforms by the Information and Library Network (INFLIBNET) Centre, UGC — essential for thesis submission, plagiarism checks, and e-resource access. As per UGC regulations, every Ph.D. thesis is deposited with INFLIBNET (Shodhganga) before the award is announced.
