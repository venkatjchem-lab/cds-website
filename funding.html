Research Funding & Grants

Funding agencies, fellowships, and grant schemes available to MRV doctoral scholars and supervisors.

The Centre for Research Development & Sustenance (CRDS) and the Directorate of Research support scholars and faculty in identifying funding opportunities, preparing proposals, and managing sanctioned grants. The agencies below are indicative national and international sources for doctoral and post-doctoral research.

🇮🇳 National Government Agencies

🏛️
DBT — Department of Biotechnology
Life sciences, biotech & health research grants
🏛️
ICMR — Indian Council of Medical Research
Biomedical & clinical research, JRF/SRF fellowships
🏛️
DST — Department of Science & Technology
INSPIRE, science & technology programmes
🏛️
SERB / ANRF — Science & Engineering Research Board
Core research, national post-doctoral fellowships
🏛️
CSIR — Council of Scientific & Industrial Research
JRF/SRF, research associateships
🏛️
DSIR — Department of Scientific & Industrial Research
Industrial R&D, PRISM & CRTDH support
🏛️
DAE — Department of Atomic Energy
Radiation, materials & life-sciences research
🏛️
INSA — Indian National Science Academy
Fellowships, visiting scientist & exchange grants
🏛️
DOS / ISRO — Department of Space
Space technology & applications research

💠

💠

💠
Ratan Tata Trust & Navajbai Ratan Tata Trust
Health, education & development research grants
💠
Sree Padmavathi Venkateswara Foundation
Health & biomedical research support

🎓 Fellowships for Scholars

🎯
UGC-NET JRF / UGC-CSIR NET JRF
Junior Research Fellowship for qualified scholars
🎯
ICMR / DBT JRF
Fellowships for biomedical & life-sciences scholars
🎯
SERB National Post-Doctoral Fellowship (N-PDF)
Early-career post-doctoral support
🎯
MRV Institutional Seed Funding
Internal seed grants via CRDS & MR-Foundation
How to apply: Scholars should route funding applications through their Research Supervisor and the Directorate of Research / CRDS. Contact the CDS office at phd@mrvv.edu.in for proposal-development support, institutional endorsements, and grant-management guidance.

Indicative list compiled for reference. Scheme availability, eligibility, and deadlines are set by the respective funding agencies and may change — always confirm on the official agency portal.
