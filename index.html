Why CDS

Research-First Environment

🏛️
UGC-Compliant Programs

Ph.D. programs framed per UGC 2022 norms and the MRV Academic Regulation 2025, at a UGC Section-3 Deemed University.

🤝
Expert Guidance

Mentorship from 67 recognized research supervisors across 8 faculties spanning the health sciences and technology.

🔬
Translational Research

Clinical research, central instrumentation, and interdisciplinary collaboration through CRDS, MRRC & the Clinical Research Unit.

🚀
Funding & Innovation

Seed funding, grant facilitation, patent & tech-transfer support, and an incubation centre via the MR-Foundation.
