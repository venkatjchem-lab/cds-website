Ph.D. Programs

Doctoral research across 8 faculties — 67 supervisors, 203 active scholars (2025 & 2026 batches).

Note: All Ph.D. programs are available as full-time and part-time, conducted as per UGC (Minimum Standards and Procedures for Award of Ph.D. Degree) Regulations, 2022. Minimum duration: 3 years (full-time), 5 years (part-time).
